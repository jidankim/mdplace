# Work Journal, Scheduler, mdplace Agent, and Control Channel contract

This Normative Material defines the non-semantic control plane for one configured vault. It consumes current Semantic Kernel truth, schedules bounded work, and recovers interrupted operations. It cannot establish semantic truth, accept a Placement Outcome, change the Category Tree, authorize Folder Projection semantics, append an OperationCommit, or bypass the Vault Mutation Gate. The contracts and conformance executables in this ticket specify behavior only; they are not production mdplace code.

## REQ-CP-001: Operational state never establishes semantic truth

The Work Journal, Scheduler, mdplace Agent lifecycle, Readiness Gate, Work Lease, Control Channel, Control Command, Child Work Invocation, retry state, cancellation state, queue state, and Work Recovery are operational. Every closed control-plane document declares `semantic_authority` as `none`, and every stateful Conformance Fixture observes an unchanged Semantic Kernel state digest. A control-plane request to establish, alter, or override semantic truth is denied and may only be resubmitted as a command to the Semantic Kernel under its own authority, exact bases, and preconditions.

The control plane may read current semantic references as inputs and may schedule a Semantic Kernel command, but neither successful scheduling nor recovery is semantic acceptance. A Work Journal receipt is never an OperationCommit or proof that an intended semantic effect occurred.

## REQ-CP-002: Work Journal and Scheduler records are closed durable and bounded

The Work Journal is durable before a Work Item becomes eligible. Its head sequence is the last contiguous durable receipt sequence and its head digest is the canonical digest of that complete ordered receipt chain; a caller-selected head label, a gap, or a digest over different receipts is invalid. A stateful scenario may compact an unrelated prior chain into one keyed journal-prefix checkpoint, but every represented lifecycle receipt must then be the exact contiguous suffix after that checkpoint, the EnqueueReceipt must name the checkpoint as its original base, and the current keyed head must be derived from the checkpoint and complete suffix. The checkpoint also authenticates the complete active-lease summary carried forward from that compacted prefix. Stateful scenarios carry one independent vault identity plus that checkpoint, a keyed journal-head receipt, and a keyed dependency-state receipt over the complete versioned dependency snapshot; the Work Item repeats that exact dependency snapshot and its semantic-head entry must equal the independently replayed Semantic Kernel version and digest. The observer requires the Agent, journal, channel, owner authorization, writer, Scheduler lease evidence, readiness evidence, Work dependencies, and semantic head to share those derived bases before comparing an action base. Each Work Item binds one stable identity, version, kind, idempotency key, exact input digest, versioned and digest-bound dependencies, queue state, budget, retry count, durable retry-eligibility tick, recovery-interruption count, rejection count, resume count, one keyed EnqueueReceipt, optional Work Lease, immutable cancellation and completion histories, optional keyed ResumeReceipt, and an optional current terminal result. The EnqueueReceipt binds the Work Item identity, initial version, idempotency key, input digest, original pre-enqueue Work Journal head sequence and digest, and its own next journal sequence to exactly one durable enqueue entry. Every cancellation history entry binds its exact identity, version, idempotency key, requester, sequence, observation tick, reason, resume counters, applicable lease, and the authenticated Vault Owner `cancel` authorization receipt to exactly one cancellation entry. A resumed Work Item preserves that immutable entry and its adjacent terminal completion, and requires a durable keyed ResumeReceipt binding the cancelled and resumed versions, authenticated resume authorization, original cancellation identity and signature, original cancellation-completion identity and signature, exact counter increment, idempotency key, and its next distinct journal sequence to exactly one durable resume entry. Every Work Lease repeats the exact Work Item identity and version, owner, acquired and expiry ticks, and active, revoked, or expired status. Every completion-history result repeats the exact Work Item identity and version, applicable lease, idempotency key, original pre-completion Work Journal head sequence and digest, journal sequence, completion tick, signer, output or failure code, durable failure retryability and timing basis when applicable, signature scheme, trusted signing-key identity, and authenticated signature digest. It corresponds to exactly one durable completion entry with the same receipt identity, sequence, Work Item identity and version, applicable lease identity, and terminal state. Cancellation and terminal completion are distinct durable entries with distinct contiguous journal sequences. All enqueue, lease, start, retry, cancellation, resume, completion, rejection, and recovery entries carry keyed authentication over every behavior-bearing field and replay through one exact versioned Work Item state chain. Embedded enqueue, cancellation, resume, and completion receipts correspond bidirectionally to exactly one generic entry; every other admitted entry must advance that authenticated state chain and its durable counter exactly as declared. Orphan, duplicate, unauthenticated, out-of-order, or contextually impossible lifecycle records are invalid. A terminal result with a lease must correlate to the latest durable lease and start receipt before completion for the same Work Item attempt; executing failure, cancellation, completion, and recovery ticks must be at or after that authenticated start and strictly before expiry except an expiry-driven recovery tick. Cancellation uses the same applicable lease as its owner authorization and may have no lease only when it precedes dispatch. A nonterminal Work Item has no non-cancellation completion history. Success requires an output digest and no failure code; cancellation requires exactly `control.cancelled`; failure permits only `control.execution_failed`, `control.retry_tick_overflow`, `control.retry_ceiling_exceeded`, or `control.recovery_ceiling_exceeded`, with ceiling codes accepted only when the corresponding durable counter is exhausted and retry-tick overflow accepted only from its authenticated retryable failure tick plus the exact selected delay exceeding the final dispatch tick. A terminal result is accepted only after keyed verification against validator-owned signer-key-to-Agent trust material, with signer and key identities inside the authenticated payload; paired signer and binding relabels are rejected. Unknown fields, duplicate identities, noncontiguous sequences, idempotency bindings, contradictory lifecycle ownership, malformed digests, invalid authentication, or budgets outside the schema are rejected before queue mutation.

The v1 public limits are at most 500 Work Journal receipt sequences, three total attempts (an initial attempt plus a retry ceiling of two), 900,000 milliseconds wall time per attempt, 10,485,760 output bytes, one Child Work Invocation per Work Item, eight concurrently executing Work Items, four concurrent Child Work Invocations, a 300-tick Work Lease, and two recovery interruptions before terminal failure. The sequence ceiling equals the public schema's maximum complete receipt inventory and remains representable inside the validator's fixed collection, operation, and file-size budgets; every one- or two-entry transition reserves its exact remaining capacity before mutation. The retry delay schedule is exactly 1,000 then 5,000 milliseconds, represented by the same number of one-millisecond conformance ticks. A retry-eligibility tick is recorded only when the subsequent 300-tick Work Lease can still fit in the closed tick domain; otherwise the Work Item terminalizes with an authenticated failure receipt that binds retryability, the observed failure tick, and the selected delay. A policy or Work Item may tighten any limit but cannot exceed it. Exhausted time, output, process, concurrency, attempt, or recovery budgets fail closed.

The Scheduler reads only committed Work Journal state. Its durable instance must name the current persistent Agent and exact vault, that vault must match the Work Journal and Agent vault, it must exactly match the Work Journal head sequence and digest, reproduce every eligible Work Item identity, version, input digest, and eligibility tick, and reproduce every active Work Lease field without omissions or additions at its declared observation tick. Its per-claim capacity decision is bound to a canonical snapshot digest over the complete current active-lease identity set and concurrency ceiling. Stateful scenarios carry one keyed complete Scheduler active-lease receipt inventory; its exact sorted identities, count, and fields must equal the still-active lease summary authenticated by the journal-prefix checkpoint plus the represented suffix Work Item at that observation tick. A modeled leased or executing Work Item must match its corresponding receipt exactly, and an orphan, expired, or uncorrelated Scheduler lease is invalid. A dispatch first publishes a version-bound Work Lease receipt and only then acknowledges dequeue. No in-memory queue, child-process state, socket response, cache, or derived view may substitute for the committed Work Journal record.

## REQ-CP-003: Queue retry cancellation and resume are version-bound

Enqueue is idempotent only when the same idempotency key resolves to the same Work Item identity and exact input digest under the authenticated original EnqueueReceipt. A replay repeats the original pre-enqueue journal base from that receipt and returns it without appending, even when the current journal head has advanced; a different binding is rejected. Dispatch requires the current Work Journal and semantic dependency to be available, the local same-user Control Channel to be open, a current queued or retry-eligible Work Item version, the exact Work Journal head sequence and digest, exact dependency-state digest, exact digest-bound Scheduler active-lease snapshot, capacity below the eight-work concurrency ceiling, a keyed retained-writer receipt at the current epoch, the complete ordered keyed readiness chain, and a current tick at or after the durable retry-eligibility tick. A new 300-tick lease may be acquired only when its expiry remains inside the closed tick domain, and any journal-appending transition requires remaining head-sequence capacity. Exactly one live Work Lease owns a Work Item. A failure or acknowledgement requires the exact active lease and a current tick strictly before its expiry. A stale Work Item version, journal head, dependency digest, Scheduler snapshot, writer epoch or receipt, readiness chain, revoked or expired Work Lease, early retry, exhausted concurrency capacity, duplicate owner, or expired base is rejected without changing state.

A retryable failure durably increments the retry count and records the next bounded delay before the Work Item becomes eligible. A `retry_wait` item whose count equals two represents the scheduled second and final retry and may dispatch once at or after its eligibility tick; a failure or interrupted execution during that attempt appends an authenticated terminal-failure result, including the exact failure code, and any later retry is illegal. Retry scheduling that would exceed the closed tick range also appends terminal failure rather than constructing an out-of-schema state. Direct `complete_work` is legal only from executing and binds the exact active Work Lease, Work Item version, current Work Journal head, idempotency key, output digest, and completion tick inside the authenticated success receipt. Repeating that exact binding against the stored terminal version returns the original authenticated CompletionReceipt without another append; a different prior head, idempotency key, lease, output, or completion tick is denied. Cancellation before dispatch or during execution is durable, takes precedence over new dispatch and retry, and remains cancelled after restart. Cancellation and resume require the current local same-user Control Channel plus a keyed vault-owner authorization receipt binding the owner principal, vault, action kind, exact Work Item version, applicable current Work Lease or explicit absence, and idempotency key. A lease-bearing cancellation with a stale, omitted, or different lease is denied. Repeating cancellation against the exact cancelled version and idempotency key returns only the authenticated structured original cancellation receipt, whose signature also binds its requester and resume counters, without another mutation. A new cancellation reserves and appends two distinct sequences: the cancellation receipt first and terminal completion second. Cancellation during execution records both before terminating Child Work Invocation capability. Resume additionally requires the exact cancelled version and remaining resume budget. V1 allows one resume; it appends a keyed receipt binding the cancelled and resumed versions, original cancellation receipt and completion, idempotency key, resume count exactly one greater than the cancellation count, and journal sequence, increments the Work Item version, and returns the item to queued state without erasing cancellation history. Repeating that exact binding is legal only while the item remains at that exact resumed queued version; other states or later versions are denied. A later cancellation preserves the prior cancellation, completion, and resume receipts and uses version-scoped receipt identities.

The complete matrices in `contracts/transitions/work-queue-lifecycle.json`, `retry-lifecycle.json`, and `cancellation-lifecycle.json` enumerate every legal and illegal state-command pair with actor authority, preconditions, bases, records, filesystem effects, idempotency, terminal result, failure result, and recovery. Conformance pins the canonical digest of every complete row, so changing any behavior-bearing field without a new contract version fails validation even if a package refreshes its self-declared artifact hash.

## REQ-CP-004: One persistent mdplace Agent becomes ready in strict order

One persistent per-vault mdplace Agent is supervised by the user's LaunchAgent. Foreground recovery hosts the same Agent core and must acquire the same Exclusive Writer Lock. A competing Agent is denied; lock possession is retained and revalidated during readiness, wake, and work claim. Lock loss stops new claims and moves the Agent to blocked recovery.

Startup, restart, and wake expose an authenticated diagnostic-only Control Channel before evaluating these gates in order; that mode admits only `status` and `doctor` and never admits work:

1. acquire the Exclusive Writer Lock;
2. verify the configured vault and bound filesystem profile;
3. validate and replay canonical Semantic Kernel state;
4. validate executable and schema compatibility;
5. replay or rebuild required disposable views;
6. reconcile the Work Journal with exact mutation plans, manifests, and receipts;
After gate six succeeds, one atomic ready commit promotes the already-authenticated channel from `diagnostic_only` to `work_admitting`.

The Agent may enter `ready` only when the keyed retained-writer receipt matches the current persistent Agent, vault, retained token, and current writer epoch, or, during acquisition, binds the exact prior and next epoch; all six gate observations occur in the canonical order with keyed receipts binding the Agent, vault, gate, ordinal, verdict, observation digest, and preceding current writer-or-gate signature; and the atomic ready commit has promoted the channel to work-admitting. A historically valid receipt from an earlier writer epoch and a patterned receipt identifier are not evidence. Restart and Work Recovery repeat the same gates; a competing writer, stale journal head, stale epoch, missing or forged receipt, broken receipt chain, reordered gate, or failed gate leaves the Agent blocked with diagnostic-only access. A blocked Agent exposes the durable reason through status and doctor but accepts no new work.

LaunchAgent supervision follows the closed `launchagent-supervision-profile.json` and `launchagent-supervision-lifecycle.json`. V1 permits at most three automatic restart attempts with the single ordered conformance schedule 1,000, 5,000, and 30,000 ticks. Unexpected exits and authenticated startup, readiness, or wake integrity failures advance the same durable failure counter and select the delay for that exact ordinal. Only ordinals one and two enter backoff; `restart_ceiling_reached` is the atomic observation of the authenticated, linked third qualifying-failure receipt and persists failure count three, the open circuit, its trip receipt, and the doctor report together before another launch can occur. A backoff state therefore always has a count strictly below the ceiling, and an owner-approved recovery failure returns to the still-open circuit rather than re-entering automatic backoff. The circuit breaker is stored in durable Agent state and forbids another automatic start. Wake revalidates the retained Exclusive Writer Lock, bound vault/filesystem profile, and filesystem drift before work admission. The durable doctor report binds the exact three-receipt failure chain: every receipt is Agent-authenticated and binds the vault, persistent Agent, ordinal, class, code, observation tick, selected delay, prior receipt signature, and circuit version; it also binds the blocked reason, six readiness observations, Work Journal head, Semantic Kernel digest, and wake observations. Leaving an open circuit requires a keyed vault-owner recovery approval bound to that exact report, vault, persistent Agent, circuit version, selected recovery action, and idempotency key; approval still grants no work, semantic, or filesystem authority and normal lock/readiness checks remain mandatory. The complete Agent, readiness, writer, channel, and supervision matrices are `contracts/transitions/agent-lifecycle.json`, `readiness-lifecycle.json`, `exclusive-writer-lifecycle.json`, `control-channel-lifecycle.json`, and `launchagent-supervision-lifecycle.json`.

## REQ-CP-005: The Control Channel is local authenticated bounded and stale-safe

The Control Channel is one Unix domain socket in a configured same-user runtime directory with directory mode `0700` and socket mode `0600`. Creating the diagnostic-only listener binds only the configured runtime path, one vault identity, and protocol version; it neither requires nor can depend on credentials from a peer that has not yet connected. Each accepted command connection verifies macOS peer credentials against the effective user and accepts no TCP, loopback HTTP, cross-user, cross-vault, remote, or ambient endpoint. The protocol version and current command version are explicit. Its closed modes are `closed`, `diagnostic_only`, `work_admitting`, and `draining`. Diagnostic-only mode accepts only authenticated `status` and `doctor`; enqueue, dispatch, cancel, resume, shutdown, child work, and every mutation-affecting command require work-admitting mode.

One request is limited to 65,536 bytes, one response to 1,048,576 bytes, five seconds, and eight concurrent requests. A Control Command binds its vault, authenticated peer, command identity, idempotency key, closed command-kind-discriminated payload, payload digest, expected command version, and the complete command-kind-specific base inventory. Status carries an empty payload; doctor carries its closed scope; enqueue carries the complete proposed Work Item identity, kind, input digest, versioned dependencies, and bounded budget; cancel carries the exact Work Item version, applicable lease or explicit absence, and reason; resume carries the exact cancelled version and explicit lease absence; and shutdown carries its graceful mode. The payload digest is recomputed from canonical JSON containing exactly `command_kind`, `command_version`, `idempotency_key`, `payload`, and the ordered complete `base_references`; a kind, version, idempotency, body, or base relabel without the matching digest is invalid. Cancel and resume carry exactly one current Work Item base, a new enqueue carries exactly one current Work Journal base, an enqueue replay carries exactly the original pre-enqueue Work Journal base authenticated by the matching EnqueueReceipt, and status, doctor, and shutdown carry none; an extra, omitted, duplicate, stale, or inapplicable base is invalid. A Work Item base repeats its identity and version and carries the canonical digest of the complete current durable Work Item; a Work Journal base repeats its journal identity and applicable head sequence as version and exact head digest. Nonlocal, unauthenticated, wrong-vault, oversized, timed-out, over-concurrency, unsupported-version, and stale commands are denied before dispatch. Successful local authentication admits a request but grants no semantic or Vault Mutation Gate authority. The complete matrix is `contracts/transitions/control-channel-lifecycle.json`.

## REQ-CP-006: Child work is isolated and disposable

Every Child Work Invocation is a fresh process for one Work Item. It receives only a policy-authorized Processing Envelope, one output schema, bounded scratch storage, a named credential reference, an exact endpoint allowlist, and the Work Item budget. It receives no vault, repository instructions, shell, browser, ambient environment, arbitrary network, mutable queue, Work Journal, Semantic Kernel writer, or Vault Mutation Gate capability.

A child crash, timeout, malformed output, budget exhaustion, attempted capability use, or attempted semantic write terminates that invocation. The closed `contracts/schemas/child-work-invocation.schema.json` contract binds the persistent Agent, exact Work Item, lease, journal head, Processing Envelope, policy version and digest, scratch boundary, credential reference without material, endpoint allowlist, budgets, denied capabilities, schema-valid non-authoritative output, fresh-process lifecycle, and completion receipt. Only a keyed receipt whose trusted signer is the bound persistent Agent and whose signature binds the exact invocation, work, version, lease, journal sequence, and output digest may affect operational completion. Absence, mismatch, competing signer, forgery, or ambiguity invokes Work Recovery and never represents success.

## REQ-CP-007: Work Recovery is default-deny and receipt-driven

Work Recovery reads the exact authenticated Work Journal head sequence and digest, exact authenticated dependency-state snapshot, Work Item version, Work Lease identity, status and ticks, idempotency binding, retry and resume budgets, durable recovery-interruption count, structured authenticated cancellation receipt, authenticated lease-correlated completion receipt, mutation plan and filesystem observations when relevant, current Exclusive Writer Lock receipt and epoch, ordered Readiness Gate receipts, and versioned semantic dependency references. Restart applies the same journal, dependency, writer, and readiness bases. Both retain diagnostic-only access while blocked and promote the Control Channel to work-admitting only after all six gates pass. They must not infer completion from process absence, queue absence, a child output file, a derived view, a Boolean completion claim, or another caller assertion.

Committed enqueue survives process loss. A leased or executing item without an authenticated terminal receipt remains recoverable in v1 only when expiry is derivable from its authenticated acquisition/start window and existing retry and recovery budgets remain. The v1 public receipt inventory has no revocation entry, so a recovery row that merely labels its lease revoked is denied; a later contract may admit revocation only with a distinct earlier authenticated durable record. An unsigned mutable lease-status label never authorizes recovery. Recovering interrupted execution consumes the same retry budget as an ordinary retry. Completed work is never repeated after restart. Cancelled work remains cancelled. Unknown physical completion is blocked for explicit reconciliation. Repeated interruption must advance the durable counter by exactly one and cannot create duplicate work; a third interruption, or interrupted execution with an exhausted retry ceiling, atomically appends an authenticated terminal-failure result and durable terminal record. The closed `contracts/control-plane/recovery-matrix.json` supplies exactly `RC-CP-001` through `RC-CP-015`; the digest-bound `conformance/evidence/control-plane-recovery-report.json` records those cases, every scenario result, and the validator-owned denied rows in all eight control-plane transition tables.

The v1 conformance executables use HMAC-SHA-256 with the fixed `signer-key:primary-001` Agent trust anchor and the distinct `signer-key:vault-owner-001` vault-owner trust anchor solely to make authentication deterministic and independently testable. The verifier owns the key-to-`agent:primary-001` and key-to-`person:owner-001` mappings, authenticates the applicable scheme, key identity, and signer identity together with every receipt field, and rejects an Agent-signed owner approval; caller-selected expected identities cannot extend either trust map. Both keys are Conformance Fixture material, not production keys or a storage design. A production implementation must provision equivalent role-separated trusted signer material outside untrusted receipt fields and may choose another authenticated-signature mechanism while preserving every bound field and rejection behavior above.

## REQ-CP-008: Exactly twenty-five stateful scenarios prove the public boundary

The Conformance Manifest owns exactly 25 stateful `CP-001` through `CP-025` scenarios under `conformance/scenarios/control-plane/`, in the order listed by issue #34: durable enqueue; receipt-before-ack dequeue; recoverable lease; first retry; retry-ceiling failure; retry-beyond-ceiling denial; pre-dispatch cancellation; in-execution cancellation; cancellation after restart; authorized bounded resume; queued restart; in-flight crash; completed restart; idempotent duplicate enqueue; single durable concurrent owner; stale Work Item version; stale Work Lease; stale Control Command; three fail-closed Readiness Gate cases; authenticated local access; unauthenticated or nonlocal denial; persistent writer acquisition and retention; and competing-writer denial.

The scenarios include positive, negative, exact-boundary, stale-state, authority-denial, illegal-transition, crash/recovery, cancellation, restart, resume, and repeated-interruption coverage. Separate artifact fixtures prove valid isolated Child Work Invocation output, denied capability escalation, forged completion-receipt rejection, and rejection of a receipt naming a competing signer; they do not add stateful `CP-*` scenarios. Assertions observe only declared inputs, outputs, ordered operations, receipts, filesystem effects, unchanged semantic-state digest, terminal state, and illegal-transition status. They never inspect a private helper, thread, database row, or implementation strategy.
